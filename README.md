# Bratislava_GameJam

The winner of The Campfire Bratislava gamejam!

Made in 32 hours.

Cave Rush is a 2D pixel art infinite runner where instead of running forward… you drill straight down. Player  controls a drill machine and move it left and right while digging deeper into the cave.

Your goal is simple: 
- go as deep as possible and get the highest score.


CONTROLS:
Use your mouse to control the direction of your drill.
After getting an active special item use spacebar to activate it.


Main mechanics :

-UPGRADES- 
As you gain more points, you unlock upgrades during the run.
Every time you reach a certain score, the game pauses and gives you a choice between three different upgrades.
You can pick only one.
These upgrades can increase your drilling speed, improve your movement control etc.

-ORES-
While drilling down, you may encounter some ores which when collected will give you some additional score points.


-OBSTACLES-
There are hard rocks in your way.
If you hit a rock three times — the run is over.
No checkpoints. No second chances.
